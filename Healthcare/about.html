About Our Project

Revolutionizing Patient-Doctor Interaction

Our system is a unique integration of Artificial Intelligence and Real-Time Voice Technology. We have developed it to ensure that healthcare is not limited only to those who can type, but also reaches people who can simply express their problems through speech.

Our AI algorithm instantly analyzes symptoms and connects patients with the appropriate doctor, helping to save valuable time in emergency situations.
